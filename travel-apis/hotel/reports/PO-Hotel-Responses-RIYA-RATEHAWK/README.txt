TravelVIP Hotel API sample responses (prod)
Vendors: RIYA, RATEHAWK
Cities: delhi, mumbai, dubai

Per city folder:
- 01-city-search.json          city SRP (unfiltered)
- 01b-city-search-{3|4|5}star.json   SRP filtered by star (when present)
- 02-hotel-detail-{3|4|5}star.json   hotel details for sampled hotel
- 03-room-details-{3|4|5}star.json   room rates for sampled hotel
